# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Shoppers browsing and buying consumer electronics online. They land on the catalog to compare products, search, and check out. Context: a desktop or mobile browser, often with a goal of finding a specific product fast.

## Product Purpose

TechFlow is a consumer-electronics storefront. It exists to help shoppers discover, compare, and purchase tech products quickly. Success is a clear, trustworthy path from browse to cart, with search and navigation that never get in the way.

## Brand Personality

Modern, energetic, reliable. Clear and confident with a lively blue accent. Speaks Spanish, keeps copy short and action-oriented.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS-cream dashboards, noisy promotional headers, gradient-text branding, and layouts where navigation or search collides with primary actions.

## Design Principles

- **Buying flow first**: navigation and search are the spine; never let chrome crowd the shopping actions.
- **Uncluttered clarity**: one job per element, generous breathing room, consistent spacing.
- **Energetic but calm**: the blue accent carries energy; surfaces stay neutral and restful.
- **Precise layout**: no overlap, no overflow, no surprises at any breakpoint.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as the baseline. Full keyboard navigation, visible focus rings, reduced-motion support, and touch targets at least 44px. Navigation must be operable via Escape and click-outside on mobile.
